The Challenge & Account Evolution

Taking over management at an initial test budget of £1,784 per month, the objective was to build a lean, high-velocity launch framework capable of self-funding aggressive long-term expansion.

By scaling systematic bid structures and keyword targeting, the account expanded into 28 active product categories, routinely managing over £100,000+ in monthly ad spend with an overall lifetime efficiency of 3.74 ROAS (26.74% ACOS).
