The Challenge & Account Evolution

Taking over account management, the primary objective was to control advertising inefficiencies, maintain a highly efficient ACOS (achieving 21.29% with a 4.70 ROAS), and build a high-velocity framework capable of self-funding aggressive growth.

By optimizing systematically structured bids, negative keyword controls, and targeted match types, the account expanded to £27,094.55 in total storefront revenue across 1,761 units ordered, while dramatically boosting overall advertising efficiency.
